AI Disclosure and Acknowledgement

In accordance with the CPT208 course policy on artificial intelligence, the following disclosure is provided:

AI tools were used for wording refinement, layout suggestions, and limited implementation support. Core design logic, research findings, user requirements, and final decisions remained team-owned.

A concise AI use log is included in the repository at /ai-logs/prompts.md. It records the main AI-assisted activities, example prompt types, problems encountered, verification steps, and reflection.

Ethical Considerations:

  • AI tools were treated as productivity aids, not as design decision-makers.
  • All portfolio content was reviewed against the team's research evidence and final feature direction.
  • No fabricated user data was used.

Brief Technical Reflection on AI Use

AI tools were used to support portfolio layout refinement, wording improvement, HTML/CSS debugging, and Git/deployment troubleshooting. They helped us organize page structure and improve visual consistency, but the core project motivation, research findings, user requirements, evaluation data, and final design decisions were produced and checked by the team.

During use, we found that AI sometimes suggested content outside our final scope, such as extra coaching or badge features, or mixed Research, Users, and Ideation content together. We solved this by checking all AI-assisted output against the CPT208 requirements, our poster data, and our final three features: personalized route recommendation, DIY route creation, and community sharing.

We learned that AI is useful for scaffolding and debugging, but its output must be reviewed carefully. We did not use AI to fabricate user feedback, survey results, or evaluation data.
