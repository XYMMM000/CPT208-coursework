E01 Evaluation Process

Evaluation Process

Our evaluation followed a staged process: users first completed an onboarding questionnaire, then completed a baseline task without ClimbQuest, interacted with the ClimbQuest prototype, and finally provided feedback on how the climbing experience changed.

OnboardingRandomized questionnaire survey.
Baseline TaskUsers completed the climbing experience through traditional methods without ClimbQuest.
System InteractionUsers interacted with ClimbQuest.
Evaluation & FeedbackUsers discussed how the climbing experience changed after using the system.
E02 Evaluation Focus

Evaluation Focus from User Journey

The user journey map helped us identify key moments where climbers experience uncertainty or frustration: route recognition, tool search, product discovery, route design, completion, and sharing. These moments shaped the evaluation focus of ClimbQuest.

Route Choice Clarity

Can ClimbQuest help users find routes that match their current level and preferences?

Motivation and Achievement

Can ClimbQuest make users feel more motivated after completing or attempting a route?

Feedback and Sharing

Can ClimbQuest support post-climb reflection, recording, and community interaction?

E05 Key Findings

Key Findings

What Worked Well

  1. Personalization was highly valued.
    Personalization scored 4.91 / 5, showing that users valued route guidance adapted to their needs.
  2. The app experience was smooth.
    Usability scored 4.94 / 5, suggesting that users found the interaction flow clear and easy to use.
  3. Users showed adoption potential.
    Adoption intention scored 4.45 / 5, indicating promising willingness to continue using or recommending the app.

Areas for Improvement

  1. Recommendation support needs clearer explanation.
    AI assistance accuracy scored 3.82 / 5, lower than personalization and usability.
  2. Engagement can be strengthened.
    Engagement scored 3.97 / 5, suggesting that long-term motivation and community interaction need further refinement.
  3. Community sharing needs trust and safety design.
    Since ClimbQuest includes user-generated routes, future versions need clearer safety guidance and moderation.

E10 Final Reflection

Final Evaluation Reflection

The evaluation suggests that ClimbQuest improves the perceived climbing experience, especially in personalization and usability. Users responded positively to route guidance that felt relevant to their needs, and the mobile-first flow was easy to use. However, the lower scores for AI assistance accuracy and engagement show that the system should better explain recommendations and strengthen community-based motivation. Overall, the evaluation supports the value of ClimbQuest while identifying clear directions for future refinement.
